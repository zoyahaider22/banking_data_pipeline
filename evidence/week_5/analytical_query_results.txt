Q1. Transaction count and total amount by branch

SQL:
-- Analytical queries against database/analytics.db
-- Grain of fact_transaction: one row = one banking transaction.
-- amount is always positive. total_amount is gross volume (money in + money out).
-- net_cash_flow = credits minus debits (CREDIT adds, DEBIT subtracts).

-- Q1. Transaction count and total amount by branch
SELECT
    b.branch_id,
    b.branch_name,
    COUNT(*) AS transaction_count,
    ROUND(SUM(f.amount), 2) AS total_amount,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN f.transaction_type = 'CREDIT' THEN f.amount ELSE -f.amount END), 2) AS net_cash_flow
FROM fact_transaction AS f
JOIN dim_branch AS b ON b.branch_id = f.branch_id
GROUP BY b.branch_id, b.branch_name
ORDER BY b.branch_id

Results:
branch_id | branch_name | transaction_count | total_amount | net_cash_flow

BR002 | North Branch | 6 | 1355.0 | 1075.0

Q7. Our own question: which accounts have more money going out than coming in?

SQL:
-- Q7. Our own question: which accounts have more money going out than coming in?
SELECT
    a.account_id,
    a.account_type,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN f.transaction_type = 'CREDIT' THEN f.amount ELSE 0 END), 2) AS total_credits,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN f.transaction_type = 'DEBIT' THEN f.amount ELSE 0 END), 2) AS total_debits,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN f.transaction_type = 'CREDIT' THEN f.amount ELSE -f.amount END), 2) AS net_cash_flow
FROM fact_transaction AS f
JOIN dim_account AS a ON a.account_id = f.account_id
GROUP BY a.account_id, a.account_type
HAVING net_cash_flow < 0
ORDER BY net_cash_flow, a.account_id

Results:
account_id | account_type | total_credits | total_debits | net_cash_flow
A3002 | SAVINGS | 0.0 | 310.0 | -310.0
A1002 | SAVINGS | 0.0 | 160.5 | -160.5
A2007 | CHECKING | 0.0 | 85.0 | -85.0

INTERPRETATIONS
(total_amount is gross volume - credits plus debits added together - it is not a balance. net_cash_flow = credits minus debits.)

Q1. Lake Branch is the busiest: 8 transactions and 2450.0 gross (1780.0 net). Downtown and North have 6 each. All three branches have positive net cash flow.

Q2. There are 11 credits worth 4060.0 and 9 debits worth 675.5. Money in is about six times money out. The two totals add up to 4735.5 gross, but net cash flow is 3384.5.

Q3. Four customers have 4 transactions each and two have 2. C1004 (Noah Williams) is the only customer with negative net cash flow (-85.0): debits and no credits. C1005 has the largest credits (1275.0).

Q4. Every account has exactly 2 transactions, so the ranking is driven by amount alone: A3001 is highest at 1275.0 and A2007 lowest at 85.0.

Q5. 2026-09-06 (the original branch files) holds 10 of the 20 transactions. The three daily files add 4, 3 and 3. Net cash flow is positive on every date and lowest on 2026-09-08 (40.0).

Q6. PREMIUM customers appear only at Lake and North Branch (8 transactions, 2855.0). RETAIL has 12 transactions worth 1880.5 across all three branches. Downtown Branch is entirely RETAIL.

Q7. Three accounts have more money going out than coming in: A3002 (-310.0), A1002 (-160.5) and A2007 (-85.0). Each has debits and no credits in this data. This is net cash flow over the loaded transactions, not a bank balance, because opening balances are not in the data.
